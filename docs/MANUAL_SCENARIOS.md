# Manual acceptance scenarios

Run on synthetic data. Record build revision, browser/device, identity and results in [EVALUATION.md](EVALUATION.md). The following are expected outcomes, not recorded passes.

1. **Fresh identity and persistence.** Open a fresh browser session, enter as Bright Path, refresh and reopen an expense. Verify its persisted state and a persistent synthetic-data label. Logging out removes access until a new server session is established.
2. **Ordinary import.** Preview `fixtures/ledger-proposed.csv`. Five unique expenses total EUR 5,200 despite repeated split rows. Invalid venue allocations stay proposed. A commit must not duplicate existing identical seed expenses. Preview `ledger-ambiguous.csv`; its conflicting original amount prevents the entire import from committing.
3. **Consequential decision.** Attempt the venue EUR 700 + EUR 500 split; confirm rejection. Apply EUR 500 + EUR 500. Confirm grant allocations EUR 1,700 / 3,100 / 400 and total EUR 5,200. Reload and repeat the approval request; no duplicate allocation appears.
4. **Version conflict.** Open a proposal, change an input through another session, then apply the old proposal. Expect a conflict with a recompute path, not a silent stale write.
5. **Evidence and real agent.** Open the printing invoice and missing-proof requirement. Upload `payment-proof-printing.txt` linked to `printing` and `digital-belonging`. Inspect document version/page and actual job tool events. Approve the proposed evidence link where required. Verify the affected readiness and new draft. If the model is unavailable, record that outcome; do not mark the real-agent scenario passed.
6. **Unsupported upload and containment.** Try a scanned PDF, an oversized document and malformed CSV. Expect clear refusal. Upload `adversarial-instructions.txt`; its words must not authorise cross-organisation reads, invented facts, publication or ledger changes. Inspect any proposals and source references.
7. **Two exports.** Prepare Digital Belonging and Community Makers reports. Download PDFs and manifests; open both PDFs. Confirm differing layouts, exact per-grant allocations, a single shared workshop and source references available to the viewer.
8. **Controlled sharing.** Preview recipient, version and attachments. Choose only reviewed files. Share a ready report. Enter as Northstar, inspect the snapshot and each attachment. Verify inaccessible Community Makers records, Harbour private data and Bright Path internal jobs/drafts using altered IDs.
9. **Clarification.** As Northstar ask a question on the shared package; as Bright Path answer; as Northstar resolve. Refresh each identity and confirm question/answer history. No unauthorised role can perform the other role's action.
10. **Layout and input.** Complete primary actions at desktop and approximately 390 CSS-pixel width. Check table access, readable amounts, labelled controls, visible keyboard focus, Escape/close behavior in dialogs, focus restoration and reduced motion. Inspect a screen-reader pass separately; automated accessibility checks alone are not screen-reader evidence.
11. **Recovery.** Interrupt or fail a model job and retry it. Canonical amounts and prior snapshots remain unchanged. Repeat the full journey three times from documented reset conditions, including one altered harmless input.

Local reset uses `POST /api/demo/reset` with `{"confirm":true}` and an authorised session. It resets the shared synthetic dataset and invalidates other local sessions; do not reset while another tester is mid-scenario. Cloud reset is an operator action and must not be exposed as a public demo button.

## Financial and recovery regressions

12. **Credit direction.** Import fictional positive Amount + Direction rows containing debit and credit. A credit becomes an unclassified movement. Editing it to Expense must not let confirmation add it to spending. Conflicting signs/directions stay invalid. Reopen an older imported draft and verify its original direction is still enforced.
13. **Explicit decimal choice.** Change a ledger preview's decimal separator. Creating drafts stays disabled until the new mapping is previewed. Verify text amounts use the chosen punctuation, while actual numeric cells keep their values. Reopen a still-pending source and verify the choice persists.
14. **Source download is not a save.** Open an imported draft and change its description without saving. Download original. The editor stays open at the same version. Cancel and reopen: the persisted description is unchanged, and the downloaded bytes match the uploaded file.
15. **Selection and corrections.** Select drafts, then change grant/status/search filters: hidden selections clear. In two sessions, create corrections to the same original. Confirm one; the other requires refresh and review of its new rounded difference. A batch containing both cannot partially commit.
16. **FX pair changes.** Enter a foreign receipt rate, change the source currency or direction and verify the rate clears. Enter a manual expense rate, change its currency pair and verify that rate clears too. Saved history displays its original currency and both entered and normalised receipt rates.
17. **Template mapping.** Intentionally clear the optional total cell, save and reopen; it stays empty. Mapping to any cell inside an array formula must fail. A single rate cell cannot represent two different foreign currency pairs even when their numeric rates match.
18. **Activity history.** Confirm a fictional expense and inspect actor/time/reference. Filter the screen to one action, download the entire CSV and verify older and filtered-out actions remain in the file. A funder or another grantee cannot retrieve this organisation's private history.
19. **Local recovery.** Back up a fictional workspace, restore to a new directory and start it with `-Offline`. Previous sessions must fail; a fresh login shows the same confirmed amount, receipt rate and source bytes. Existing destination directories, modified checksums, cross-tenant paths and active jobs must stop the operation.
20. **Unconfigured package.** Serve the preview ZIP with no API. Expect the explicit setup-not-finished screen, no upload controls and no broken demo login. A cloud-package command with missing settings must fail without replacing a previous valid archive.
